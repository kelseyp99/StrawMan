Deleting Your Data in StrawMan

StrawMan allows you to delete your data at any time directly in the app.
When you delete a poll, vote, or entry, it is removed from your device’s local database (Realm) and also deleted from our cloud database (Firebase).

You do not need to contact support to request deletion.
If you have trouble accessing the app and still want data deleted, you may contact us at lifelog42@gmail.com.

For more information, see our Privacy Policy.
